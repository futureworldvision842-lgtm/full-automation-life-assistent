# how colonial education was designed to make clerks not owners (afkaar)

TITLE — Clerk Factory Ya School?

HOOK (0-2s) — Stop scrolling! Kya aap ko pata hai aap ka school aap ko 'Leader' nahi, ek obedient 'Clerk' banane ke liye design kiya gaya tha?

SCRIPT — 
Suno dost! Yeh koi conspiracy theory nahi, balkay history hai. 

1835 mein, Lord Macaulay ne British parliament ko ek policy di jise "Macaulay’s Minute" kehte hain. Unka saaf maqsad tha ke sub-continent mein ek aisi class khari ki jaye jo "dekhne mein toh hum jaisi ho, par dimaag aur soch mein bilkul Angrez." 

Unhein free-thinkers ya baaghi nahi chahiye the. Unhein chahiye the saste, obedient nokar jo chup-chaap unke ledgers likhein. Jaise anime *One Piece* mein World Government logom ko sirf orders follow karna sikhati hai, bilkul waise hi hamare schools ne humein 9-to-5 ke liye train kiya. Subah bell bajna, lines mein khade hona, aur sawal na poochne ki aadat—ye sab dimaagi ghulami ki walls hain. 

Muhammad Qureshi ka mission isi system ko badalna hai—humein 'Clerks' nahi, 'Owners' aur azaad thinkers banna hoga.
